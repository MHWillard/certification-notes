Q: Who controls the data is called:
A: Data ownership

Q: Where data is stored is called:
A: Data residency

Q: What laws govern data us called:
A: Data sovereignty

Q: Laws requiring data to stay in the country is called:
A: Data localization

Q: Technical choice to keep data near users for performance is called:
A: Data locality

Q: Identifying internal data and understanding the impact of security breaches is called:
A: Data classification

Q: Litigation, contractual, and regulatory are types of:
A: Data retention holds

Q: What principle dictates that users should be given the minimum level of access needed for their tasks?
A: Principle of Least Privilege

Q: What principle dictates that no devices should be trusted, even internal ones, without authentication?
A: Zero Trust Cloud Security

Q: When you remove unnecessary components in a system, and use the most current versions of what remains, what type of security measure is this?
A: Hardening

Q: What type of encryption uses one key?
A: Symmetric encryption

Q: What type of encryption uses a seperate key for both sender and receiver?
A: Asymmetric encryption

Q: What type of cryptographic method uses hash functions?
A: Hash cryptography

Q: Authentication information such as passwords, SSH keys, certificate keys, and API tokens are called:
A: Secrets

Q: Which type of gateway controls traffic, authentication, rate limiting, and logging activity?
A: API gateways
